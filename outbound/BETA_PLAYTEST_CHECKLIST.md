# Claudov beta playtest checklist

[Open beta](https://vincentdn.github.io/escape-from-claudov/beta/)

## Weapon Foley in a raid

- [ ] While holding a weapon, toggle safety with **U**. Check for one click on each toggle and confirm the trigger is blocked while safety is on.
- [ ] With a selectable rifle, SMG or pistol, press **B** and hold Fire. Semi-auto should fire once per press. Switch back and confirm automatic fire returns. Both mode changes should click.
- [ ] Fit a bipod to a compatible weapon. In a raid, crouch and stop moving. Listen for one deployment sound. Move, then stop again to check that it only plays on deployment.
- [ ] Reload a rifle, pistol, bolt-action sniper and pump shotgun. Listen for removal and seating, slide release, separate bolt opening and closing, and shell and pump cues.
- [ ] In the workbench, rotate a weapon and fit or remove an attachment. Check that the sounds match the action. Zoom should stay quiet.
- [ ] Hear another player or bot fire at close and long range indoors and outdoors. Check direction and distance, including when no casing is audible. Compare suppressed and unsuppressed fire where available.

## Partisan first-person view

- [ ] Compare a Partisan AK with the Riposte AK, the M16 with the M4, and the G3 or SPEAR with the SCAR-H. Check that the hip hold has a similar angle and leaves the centre of the screen clear.
- [ ] Aim down the irons on the AKs, M16, G3, MK14, SPEAR, RPK, StG variants, PPSh, Bren and Chauchat. Check that the front post appears inside the rear aperture, with space around it. Try an optic and confirm that removing it restores the irons.

## Stash and loadout

- [ ] Check that colour tiers are visible on stored items and fitted equipment, with empty slots still subdued and names readable.
- [ ] At your normal window size and a narrower window, confirm the stash grid fills its panel and remains usable when scrolling.
- [ ] Drag a multi-cell item from its far corner. The item ghost should line up with the placement outline. Rotate it with **R**, try an occupied space and an edge, then drop it into a valid space.
- [ ] Move items between the stash, carried storage and equipment. Try a stack split and a cancelled drag. Confirm counts and placement survive reload.

## Operator preview

- [ ] Open the Hideout and watch for one full idle cycle. Look for the chest carry, low carry and raised-hands gesture, with smooth transitions and a steady weapon grip.
- [ ] Change the equipped weapon, armour and backpack. Rotate the preview with the mouse and arrow keys. Check framing and clarity at your usual browser zoom.
- [ ] Enable your system's reduced-motion setting. Confirm the idle pauses and manual rotation remains available.

Attach a screenshot or short clip for visual issues, along with the weapon, location and controls used to reproduce it.
