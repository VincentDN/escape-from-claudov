# Favicon: white Kotwica and white border (chosen)

Two versions of the site icon, both in the style of the old Partisan lambda icon (red tile, dithered drop shadow, soft outline on the
emblem). `comparison.png` shows them at 16, 32, 64 and 128 pixels next to the old lambda, on a dark and a light browser tab.

- **White** (`favicon-white.svg`, PNGs at 32, 64, 256): emblem and border in white. **Chosen by the owner on 20 November 2026**
  and now in the game. The white border disappears on light browser tabs, where only the shadow outlines the tile.
- **Dark green** (`favicon-dark-green.svg`, PNGs at 32, 64, 256): emblem and border in the lambda icon's dark green. Kept for
  reference.

`SHIP` in `tools/dev/efc/brand-kotwica.mjs` picks the version; run the script again after changing it.
Both are adaptations of the CC BY-SA 4.0 Kotwica drawing credited in `NOTICE.md`.
